Bio

I am a PhD student with the Human Interaction & Robotics Group at the University of Colorado Boulder, advised by Alessandro Roncone. I am a robotics researcher, with a focus on whole-body perception and embodied learning. My contributions include procedurally generated artificial skins, a variety of whole-body tactile and proximity sensors, and information theoretic discoveries on contact anticipation for collision avoidance.

I am the primary maintainer of the GenTact Toolbox, a whole-body artificial skin generation software that makes fully 3D printable sensors. Before starting my PhD, I was an assistant researcher at Technology Applications Inc. developing thermal straps for aerospace applications.
